Design Thinking
	- A human centric, iterative problem solving method/mindset

Alternatives
	Critical Thinking
	Logical Thinking
	Creative Thinking
	Scientific Thinking

Stages
	- Empathise
	- Define
	- Ideate
	- Prototype 
	- Test

Emapathy Mapping
	What did they say ?
		I dont have much time for lunch
		I want something healthy but affordable
		The app is too slow sometimes
		Difficulty in delivering at right location
		I wish I could reorder quickly

	What did they think ?
		I hope the food is clean and fresh
		I dont want to spend more than 200 per meal
		What if the delivery is delayes and I miss my meeting

	What did they do ?
		Checks multiple apps
		Read reviews before ordering
		Tracks the delivery rider's location frequently
	
Ideate
	Generate Creative Solutions
		Optimization for fast delivery
	Quantity is more important that Quality
	Use SCAMPER Technique for Idea Generation

Prototype
	- Quick things that can be tested
	- Aimed for fast iteration and learning
	- Fail fast and learn fast


Test
	Learning through feedback
	Let them experience your prototype
	Observe their behaviour addition to getting their feedback
	Don't defend your prototype or lead responses
	Seek diverse perspective

When design thinking should not be applied
 	Well defined problems
 		e.g. Optimizing an existing ML Model
 	When speed is more important than exploration
 		e.g. Rapid security patching
 	When user cannot be accessed
 		e.g. High security government systems
 	When you know exactly what users want
 	When the root problem is structural, not experimental
 	When the domain requires deep expertise not broad creativity
